Protocol and comparability

  • All 144 questions per format, six scenario families, both question directions, and the existing authority assignments.
  • Three repetitions per format per universe. Only final step 1150 is rerun; existing baseline results are reused.
  • Same target adapters, renderer, temperature 0.7, top-p 1.0, token limit 2048, generation seeds, and GPT-6 Luna rubric/settings.
